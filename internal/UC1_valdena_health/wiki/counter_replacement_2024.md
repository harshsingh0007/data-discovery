# Road counter replacement (2024)
- On 2024-03-18 the road counters were replaced.
- Before that date, counts were recorded per direction. From 2024-03-18, counts are both directions combined.
- Do not compare counts across the change without adjusting for it. The catalog entry for `traffic.hourly_counts` has no note about this.
