# Clinic data policy for surveillance
- Patient-level visit records (`clinic.patient_visits`) may not be used for this analysis.
- Only daily counts by district (`clinic.resp_visits_daily`) may be used for this purpose.
- Patient postcode and age band are personal data. They must not leave the clinic systems for analysis.
