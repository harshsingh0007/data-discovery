# Product code change (2024)
- From 2024-04-01, product codes changed. Codes ending in "L" were replaced by new codes.
- No mapping table exists between the old and new codes.
- Old and new codes can only be matched by product name, and some names are not unique.
- `sales.products` lists only the current codes.
